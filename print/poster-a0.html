1Background

Stroke takes away movement.
Therapy is hard to reach.

Hemiparesis — weakness on one side of the body — is the most common motor effect of stroke. Recovery depends on hundreds of guided repetitions, yet therapists, clinic time and travel are limited, especially once patients return home. Most existing rehabilitation robots are large, expensive and stay in hospitals.

3AI adaptive assistance

Help only as much as needed.

τmotor = (1 − ê) · τtaskê = patient effort estimated by AI from sEMG + joint kinematics

  • A lightweight model on the ESP32 classifies muscle activity and movement into an effort score.

  • As the patient improves, assistance fades automatically — keeping therapy challenging but safe.

  • Fatigue or spasm detection raises assistance or soft-stops the actuator.
